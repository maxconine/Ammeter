Not connected

How to use

  1. 01 Open this page in a web browser on a desktop computer.
  2. 02 Plug in the Pro Micro over USB, then click Connect and select its serial port.
  3. 03 With no load current flowing, click Zero to remove sensor offset.
  4. 04 Watch the live current reading. Click Record when you want to capture a plot.
  5. 05 Click Stop to end the recording, then Export CSV if you need the data.

Live vs record. Current updates continuously after connect. Record only stores samples for the plot, peak, average, and CSV.

Zero first. On a 100 A / 75 mV shunt, 1.5 mV of offset looks like 2 A. Always Zero with the load off.

Wiring. Pro Micro pin 2 = SDA, pin 3 = SCL, VCC (not RAW), shared GND. Sense leads go across the shunt.

Bus voltage. After uploading the latest sketch, if Bus still reads 0 V, wire the INA226 VBUS pin to the 24 V supply rail (in addition to the shunt sense leads).

Buffer. Up to 6,000 samples (~10 minutes at 100 ms).
